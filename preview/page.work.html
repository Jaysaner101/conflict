How a piece gets made

One pair of hands, start to finish
  1. The artworkYour logo or one of his designs is cleaned up and sized to the item.
  2. The setupThe item is squared on the bed and the red dot traces the outline before the laser fires.
  3. The passThe industrial fiber laser cuts a deep, permanent engraving into the metal.
  4. The checkCleaned, inspected and packed by the same hands.

New designs, restocks and the apparel release. Occasional, and nothing else.
