About the show

Counter Culture Conflict is Murphy's take on the fights shaping the world: wars, unrest, policing and the politics behind them.

Each episode takes the stories the big outlets smooth over, plays the footage, and says plainly what he thinks is going on. Global perspectives, unfiltered analysis.
