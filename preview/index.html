For businesses and organisations

Custom and bulk orders

Custom designs and logos are available for larger orders: businesses, nonprofits, teams, departments and schools.

  • Your logo or your own design. Uploaded, placed and proofed before anything is engraved.
  • Twelve pieces or more. Volume pricing, and the price drops as the quantity rises.
  • Local pickup on large custom orders. Arranged once the order is approved.
  • No one-off custom orders. Single items are not engraved at the shop, and no meetings are taken for one item.
