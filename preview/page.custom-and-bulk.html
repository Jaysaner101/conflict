No one-off custom orders.

Murphy does not engrave single custom items at the shop, and does not take meetings for one item. Custom designs and logos are for larger orders of twelve pieces or more. Local pickup is available on large custom orders only, arranged after the order is approved. For a single item, order from the online store and add a name for $5.

What happens next

Proof included on every order

  2. You get a proofAn exact mockup and a firm price within 2 business days.
  3. You approve itNothing is engraved until you sign off.
  4. It shipsMost runs leave 7 to 10 business days after approval.
